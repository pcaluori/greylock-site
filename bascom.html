Semi-hollow archtop

Bascom

A large semi-hollow archtop with a woody attack and sustain that blooms — built for the player who wants the guitar to breathe with them.

Bascom is Greylock’s largest instrument, and the clearest example of the whole idea. The bigger, more resonant semi-hollow archtop body, the vintage-voiced humbucker set it carries as standard, and the feel of the instrument under your hands all point the same direction — none of it is there to be admired on its own. Together, they produce a touch sensitivity that most electrics ask you to give up.

The larger body produces a woody attack and a sustain that blooms rather than cuts off.

Bascom is built for the guitarist whose technique lives in dynamics and phrasing — the space between notes as much as the notes themselves — and who wants an electric that responds the way a great acoustic does: breathing with the player, meeting their touch rather than resisting it.

A guitar honed to disappear in your hands.

Built to order

Want a Bascom built around the way your hands move between chords and lead? Bascom can be built to order, and that conversation is exactly the one we want to have.

Bascom carries Greylock’s vintage-voiced humbucker set as standard — warm, open, and touch-sensitive, built to cover jazz through blues, rock, and rockabilly without changing character. Paired with the Bigsby, that vintage humbucker voice leans further into rockabilly territory: shimmer and pitch-wobble from the vibrato, body and bloom from the pickups underneath it.
