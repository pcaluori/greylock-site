Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

At Greylock Guitars, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you visit our website or contact us about our instruments.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, such as your name, email address, and any details you share when submitting an inquiry through our contact form. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics platforms, or third-party advertising services.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about your custom instrument order. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law.

Email Communications

If you contact us, we may follow up via email about your inquiry, build progress updates, or order details. You may opt out of any non-essential communications at any time by replying to any email from us.

Data Retention

We retain customer correspondence and order records for the duration of our business relationship.

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Our website does not store payment information — any transactions are handled through secure, third-party payment processors.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at info@greylockguitars.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us at info@greylockguitars.com.
