Semi-hollow

Goshawk

Semi-hollow, light in the hands, and as capable as its namesake — built for the player who moves between styles inside a single set.

Goshawk carries the same idea Saddleback does, tuned for a different kind of night. The chambered semi-hollow body is lighter on the shoulder for long sets and adds air and harmonic complexity to the tone without inviting feedback at stage volume — and it’s paired with electronics voiced for the same versatility, not because any one piece does the work alone, but because the weight, the wood, and the pickups were all chosen with the same player in mind.

It’s warmer than Saddleback, and it rewards a lighter touch — dig in and it still answers, but it doesn’t need force to speak.

Goshawk is built for the guitarist who moves between genres in a single gig or session — jazz into indie into a driven solo — without reaching for a different guitar each time.

A guitar honed to disappear in your hands.

Built to order

Want a Goshawk voiced differently for how you dig in? Goshawk can be built to order, and that conversation is exactly the one we want to have.

Standard format

Choice of
Balanced Power, Smoke & Oak, or Glass & Chrome
Also available
Vintage humbucker set

Because Goshawk already moves between styles, the pickup choice sets exactly how far it leans in any direction. Balanced Power keeps it open and articulate for players crossing between jazz, indie, and clean-toned genres in a single set. Smoke & Oak adds midrange body and grind for players who push further into blues rock and soul territory. Glass & Chrome leans brightest of the three — a single-coil-adjacent voice for funk, power-pop, and jangle players, with an optional on-board boost for extra signal without losing that character. The vintage humbucker set is also available for a warmer, more touch-sensitive voice.
